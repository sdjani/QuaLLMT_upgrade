Li-Fi Design & Development

Light-speed connectivity for secure, high-speed communication

Li-Fi (Light Fidelity) is a cutting-edge wireless communication technology that uses visible light from LEDs to transmit data at ultra-high speeds. Unlike traditional Wi-Fi, which relies on radio frequencies, Li-Fi leverages the visible light spectrum, offering secure, interference-free, and high-bandwidth connectivity.

Key Advantages

  • Extremely high data rates (up to several Gbps)
  • Enhanced security – light does not penetrate walls
  • No electromagnetic interference (EMI), ideal for sensitive environments
  • Unlicensed spectrum, reducing congestion and regulatory hurdles
  • Energy-efficient, leveraging existing LED lighting infrastructure

Applications

  • Hospitals and healthcare facilities (EMI-free communication)
  • Defense and secure communications
  • Smart offices and homes
  • Industrial automation and IoT
  • Underwater communication
  • Indoor navigation and positioning

Our Expertise

  • Modulation and coding schemes for visible light communication
  • Integration with existing lighting and IT infrastructure
  • Secure and reliable data transmission solutions
  • Custom software for Li-Fi network management and analytics
